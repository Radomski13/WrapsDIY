FAQs

Frequently asked questions

How do I tell you what goes on my design?
After you order you get a link to a short form. Upload your logo, add the wording you want, and send a couple of photos of your van. A designer turns that into your first proof.
What if my van has deadlocks or a rear camera?
Positions vary by van, so we send a matching template with your order. Trim the vinyl around the lock or camera for a clean fit.
Do I pay before I see a design?
Yes. Each sign is made for your van, so payment starts the design. If you are not happy by the third proof, you get a full refund.
Is it hard to fit a wrap myself?
Each panel is labeled and printed with a little extra around the edges, so you trim it for a clean finish. The air-release vinyl lets you lift and reposition it. Most people fit a set of sides in a day with the video guide. Placeholder answer.
What condition does my paint need to be in?
Factory paint in good condition works best. Avoid installing over rust, flaking lacquer or fresh paint, as the vinyl can lift or pull paint when removed. Placeholder answer.
