FAQs

Perforated vinyl questions

What is perforated window vinyl?
It’s vinyl film with thousands of tiny holes. From outside you see your full-color graphic. From inside you can still see out, like looking through a screen. It’s also called window perf or one-way vision film.
What’s the difference between 50/50 and 70/30?
The numbers are the printed area vs the holes. 50/50 is the standard and gives the best view out. 70/30 has more printed area for a bolder image, with a little less visibility.
Can I put perforated vinyl on my truck or car back window?
Yes, it’s the most common use. Choose the optically clear laminate so rain doesn’t sit in the holes and blur the view. Check your state’s rules for rear-window coverage first.
Do I need the laminate?
For vehicles and outside glass, yes. It keeps water and dirt out of the holes and protects the print. Inside-mounted or short-term graphics can skip it.
How do I measure my window?
Measure the visible glass, width then height, in inches. We add a little bleed so you can trim it to a clean edge.
Is it hard to install?
No. Clean the glass, peel the backing a little at a time and squeegee from the center out, then trim the edges. The install guide shows each step.
