FAQs

Truck wrap questions

How much does a truck wrap cost?
It depends on the size of the truck and how much you cover. We price by printed area, from $4.95 per sq ft printed and laminated. Send your truck details and we'll quote it. Printing it yourself and installing it saves the shop's labor bill.
Can I install a truck wrap myself?
Pickup half and partial wraps are a good DIY job. Box trucks are big, flat and easy to lay out, but you'll want two or three people. Every panel is labeled and comes with an install map. You can also hand the kit to a local installer.
Do you wrap box trucks?
Yes. Box trucks are one of the best wrap jobs there is: big flat sides that work like a billboard. Send us the box length and height and any doors or vents.
What about food trucks and trailers?
We print those too. Send photos and measurements and we'll quote it.
What film do you use?
Laminated cast vinyl made for vehicles. Choose WrapsDIY, Avery or 3M film.
Do I see the design before you print?
Yes. You get a proof on your truck and we print only after you approve it.
