How to put them on
  1. Wash and dry the panel. Make sure it’s steel: a fridge magnet should stick.
  2. Hold the sign by its top corners and let it touch the panel flat, with no air under it.
  3. Keep it off body lines, handles and trim.
  4. Take it off once a week, wipe both sides dry, and put it back.

FAQs

Magnetic sign questions

Will car magnets fall off on the highway?
Not when they sit on a flat, clean steel panel. The whole magnet should lie flat with no gaps at the edges, so wind can’t get under it. Avoid body lines, handles and trim. That’s why we only show sizes that fit a flat area on your vehicle.
Will a magnetic sign stick to my truck?
Only to steel. Some trucks have aluminum body panels, and magnets won’t stick to those. That includes the Ford F-150 (2015 and newer), Ford Super Duty (2017 and newer) and the doors on the Chevy Silverado 1500 and GMC Sierra 1500 (2019 and newer). Test with a fridge magnet first. If it doesn’t stick, a truck wrap or decals are the better fit.
Will magnets damage my paint?
No, if you look after them. Take them off once a week, wipe the magnet and the door, and let both dry. This stops dirt and moisture getting trapped underneath. Don’t put them on fresh paint or go through a car wash with them on.
What size magnet do I need?
Pick your vehicle and where the sign goes above, and we show only the sizes that fit. The largest one is marked “Best fit.” We check the fit again on your proof.
Can I use them on a personal vehicle?
Yes. That’s the main reason people buy them. Put them on for work and take them off after hours.
Do I see the design first?
Yes. A designer lays out your sign and sends a proof. Nothing prints until you approve it.
